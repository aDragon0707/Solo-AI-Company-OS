# 创始人日志模板

**目的：** 创始人思考、决策和回顾的可复制模板。

---

## 1. 原始思考模板

```markdown
## YYYY-MM-DD

### What I Am Thinking
- ...

### What I Am Worried About
- ...

### What I Want To Test
- ...

### What I Need AI To Help With
- ...

### What Is Not Decided Yet
- ...

### Should This Become A Formal Decision?
- No / Yes, move to `FOUNDER_Decision_Log.md`
```

---

## 2. 草稿决策模板

```markdown
### DRAFT-XXX - Draft Title

**Date:**
**Possible Decision:**
**Founder Leaning:**
**Reason:**
**Affected AI / Files / Customers:**
**Still Unclear:**
**Confirmed For Formal Log:** No / Yes
```

---

## 3. 正式决策模板

```markdown
### DEC-YYYYMMDD-XXX - Decision Title

**Date:** YYYY-MM-DD
**Status:** active / superseded / paused
**Decision:**

**Reason:**

**Applies To:**

**Required AI Actions:**

**Prohibited Actions:**

**Review Date:**
```

---

## 4. 回顾模板

```markdown
## YYYY-MM-DD Founder Retrospective

### What Happened
- ...

### Most Important Judgment Today
- ...

### What Went Right
- ...

### What May Be Wrong
- ...

### AI Employee Performance
- AI-01:
- AI-02:
- AI-03:
- AI-04:
- AI-05:

### Tomorrow's Most Important Action
- ...

### Should Anything Become A Decision?
- No / Yes
```
