# Founder Thinking Inbox

**Purpose:** Store raw founder thoughts, instincts, worries, ideas, and questions before they become formal decisions.

**Rule:** This file is allowed to be messy. It can contain contradictions, half-formed opinions, and unresolved tension. It is not a formal decision source.

Formal decisions must be moved into `FOUNDER_Decision_Log.md`.

---

## How To Use This File

Write here when:

- you have an idea but are not ready to decide
- you feel tension but cannot yet explain it
- you want an AI employee to investigate something
- you suspect an old plan may be wrong
- you need to separate raw thought from confirmed instruction
