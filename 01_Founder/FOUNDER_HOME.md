# Founder Home

**Purpose:** Central entry point for founder thinking, decisions, and retrospectives.

**Principle:** Founder judgment is the highest-priority source for company direction. AI worklogs record execution. They do not replace founder decisions.

---

## 1. The Three Founder Ledgers

| Ledger | File | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| Thinking Inbox | `FOUNDER_Thinking_Inbox.md` | Raw ideas, worries, instincts, questions, and unresolved thoughts |
| Decision Log | `FOUNDER_Decision_Log.md` | Confirmed decisions that AI employees must follow |
| Retrospective Log | `FOUNDER_Retrospective_Log.md` | Daily or weekly review of judgment, mistakes, lessons, and next actions |

---

## 2. Daily Use

### Start of Day

Open:

```text
01_Founder/FOUNDER_Thinking_Inbox.md
```

Write:

- what is on your mind
- what you are worried about
- what you want AI to handle
- what is not decided yet

### When Making Decisions

Open:

```text
01_Founder/FOUNDER_Decision_Log.md
```

If the decision is not ready, put it in the draft decision inbox.

If it is confirmed, write it as a formal `DEC-YYYYMMDD-XXX` entry.

### End of Day

Open:

```text
01_Founder/FOUNDER_Retrospective_Log.md
```

Record:

- what happened
- what judgment changed
- where you were right
- where you may be wrong
- how each AI employee performed
- what matters most tomorrow

---

## 3. Mandatory AI Rule

Before important work, AI employees must read:

```text
01_Founder/FOUNDER_Decision_Log.md
```

If the decision log conflicts with old dashboards, old worklogs, or prior plans, the decision log wins.

AI must flag the conflict. It must not quietly follow stale instructions.
