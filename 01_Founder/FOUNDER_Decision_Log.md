# Founder Decision Log

**Purpose:** Record confirmed founder decisions.

**Highest rule:** The latest confirmed founder decision overrides old dashboards, old AI worklogs, prior plans, draft documents, and chat history.

**Update rule:** Only write a formal decision after the founder clearly confirms it. AI may draft a proposed decision, but it must not quietly promote a draft into an active decision.

## Draft Decision Inbox

Use this area for decisions that are not yet confirmed.

Drafts are not binding. AI employees may reference them as context, but they must not treat them as orders.

## Active Decisions

### DEC-YYYYMMDD-001 - Establish Founder Decision Authority

**Date:** YYYY-MM-DD
**Status:** active

**Decision:**
The founder decision log is the single source of truth for confirmed company direction.

**Reason:**
AI worklogs and dashboards describe execution state. They do not own strategy, risk tolerance, customer promises, or public narrative.

**Applies To:**
All AI employees, dashboards, customer materials, project plans, research notes, and operating documents.

**Required AI Actions:**
- Read this file before important work.
- Surface conflicts between this file and older sources.
- Ask for founder confirmation when a decision is unclear.
- Record any completed task in the appropriate worklog.

**Prohibited Actions:**
- Do not treat a draft as a confirmed decision.
- Do not use an old AI worklog to override a newer founder decision.
- Do not make customer promises, pricing commitments, public claims, or strategic pivots without founder approval.
- Do not hide uncertainty to make the company look more advanced.

**Review Date:** YYYY-MM-DD
