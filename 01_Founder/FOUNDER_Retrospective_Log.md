# Founder Retrospective Log

**Purpose:** Record daily or weekly founder reflection.

**Principle:** A retrospective is not a victory report. It is a place to record how judgment changed, what was wrong, what was learned, and what must happen next.

---

## Retrospective Template

```markdown
## YYYY-MM-DD Founder Retrospective

### What Happened
- ...

### Most Important Judgment Today
- ...

- ...

### What May Be Wrong
- ...

### AI Employee Performance

- AI-02:
- AI-03:
- AI-04:
- AI-05:

### Tomorrow's Most Important Action
- ...

### Should Anything Become A Decision?
- No / Yes
- If yes, proposed decision:
```
