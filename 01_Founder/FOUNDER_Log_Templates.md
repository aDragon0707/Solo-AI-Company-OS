# Founder Log Templates

**Purpose:** Copyable templates for founder thinking, decisions, and retrospectives.

---

## 1. Raw Thinking Template

```markdown
## YYYY-MM-DD

### What I Am Thinking
- ...

### What I Am Worried About
- ...

### What I Want To Test
- ...

### What I Need AI To Help With
- ...

### What Is Not Decided Yet
- ...

### Should This Become A Formal Decision?
- No / Yes, move to `FOUNDER_Decision_Log.md`
```

---

## 2. Draft Decision Template

```markdown
### DRAFT-XXX - Draft Title

**Date:**

**Founder Leaning:**
**Reason:**
**Affected AI / Files / Customers:**
**Still Unclear:**
**Confirmed For Formal Log:** No / Yes
```
