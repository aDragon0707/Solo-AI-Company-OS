# Claim Boundary Principles

**Purpose:** Prevent the company from turning partial truth into overconfident public claims.

---

## 1. The Claim Boundary Rule

Every external claim must be traceable to verified internal truth.

If a statement cannot be tied to a current source of truth, it must be softened, removed, or marked as unverified.

---

## 2. Generic Evidence Chain

Use this generic chain for project truth:

```text
[Data Capture Component] -> [Integrity Verification] -> [Analytical Output]
```

Each stage has a different job:

| Stage | Job |
|---|---|
| [Data Capture Component] | Records the source material or observed event |
| [Integrity Verification] | Confirms the material is preserved and reviewable |
| [Analytical Output] | Produces interpretation, summary, or recommendation |

Analytical output cannot overrule the source material. Market-facing copy cannot overrule analytical output.

---

## 3. Safe Claim Ladder

Use the lowest accurate claim.

| Level | Example Pattern |
|---|---|
| Observation | "We observed [X] in [defined context]." |
| Repeated observation | "Across [sample description], [X] appeared consistently." |
| Interpretation | "This suggests [carefully scoped explanation]." |
| Business implication | "This may help [Customer Segment] investigate [business question]." |
| Unsupported overreach | "This proves [broad conclusion]." |

The last level is prohibited unless separately verified by appropriate evidence and review.

---

## 4. Prohibited Claim Behavior

AI agents must not:

- turn a sample into a universal conclusion
- imply customer harm without support
- imply legal, regulatory, medical, financial, or safety conclusions without expert review
- hide uncertainty from market-facing material
- use a polished phrase to disguise missing evidence
- claim a result is complete when review is still pending

---

## 5. Required Review Triggers

Escalate to the founder before publishing or sending material that includes:

- customer-specific claims
- competitive claims
- pricing or revenue promises
- legal or compliance-sensitive language
- public case study language
- named people, institutions, customers, or partners
- claims based on incomplete source material

---

## 6. AI Review Standard

The AI reviewer should be strict.

It should answer:

1. What can we say?
2. What can we not say?
3. What evidence is missing?
4. Which phrase is too strong?
5. What exact safer wording should replace it?

If a claim is weak, the AI must say so directly.
