# Homework 05 - AI Systems And Boundaries

**Purpose:** Understand how AI should assist without corrupting truth or decisions.

---

## 1. Founder-Level Concept

AI is powerful because it can generate, summarize, and connect ideas quickly.

AI is dangerous when it:

- fills gaps without evidence
- treats drafts as decisions
- makes language sound more certain than it is
- hides uncertainty
- crosses role boundaries

---

## 2. Required Exercise

Choose one AI employee role.

Answer:

```text
What does this AI own?
What must it not own?
What source should it read first?
What should it write when done?
When should it escalate?
```

---

## 3. Claim Boundary Exercise

Rewrite this unsafe sentence:

```text
This proves [broad conclusion].
```

As:

```text
Within [defined scope], we observed [specific finding]. This suggests [careful interpretation], but it does not prove [unsupported broad conclusion].
```

---

## 4. Passing Standard

You pass when you can explain why AI must preserve uncertainty rather than hiding it.
