# Homework 03 - Computer Organization And Integrity

**Purpose:** Understand why source material must be preserved and reviewable.

---

## 1. Founder-Level Concept

Computers store material as files, records, and references.

The founder-level question is:

```text
Can we later prove what the system saw, produced, or changed?
```

---

## 2. Required Exercise

Pick one output or artifact in My AI Collaboration OS.

Answer:

```text
What source material produced it?
Where is the output stored?
How do we know it is current?
How do we know it was not silently replaced?
What downstream claim depends on it?
```

---

## 3. Integrity Rule

Source truth should remain reviewable.

If the company cannot show where a conclusion came from, the conclusion should not become market-facing language.

---

## 4. Passing Standard

You pass when you can explain why preserving source context is a business discipline, not just an engineering detail.
