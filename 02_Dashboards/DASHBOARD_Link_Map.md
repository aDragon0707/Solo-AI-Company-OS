## Link Maintenance Rules

- Prefer Obsidian wiki links for internal vault navigation: `[[folder/file|Readable Label]]`.
- Keep GitHub-facing docs readable with normal paths when needed.
- Every important note should link to at least one parent map, dashboard, or ledger.
- Do not add links that imply a decision, fact, or dependency that is not present in the note.
- Do not rewrite substance while linking. Linking is navigation work, not strategy work.
- Follow [[00_System_Brain/AI_Text_Maintenance_Protocol|AI Text Maintenance Protocol]] for any dashboard, MOC, worklog-index, or handoff maintenance.
