## 4. Positioning Rules

Say:

- "We help review [Business Problem] with structured evidence."
- "We preserve source context and uncertainty."
- "We provide scoped findings and next-step recommendations."

Do not say:

- "We prove broad market-wide conclusions from a narrow sample."
- "We guarantee a specific outcome."
- "We can replace expert review where expert review is required."

---

## 5. Strategy Review Questions

Each month, answer:

1. Which customer segment responded best?
2. Which claim caused confusion?
3. Which source-truth gap blocked sales?
4. Which deliverable created the most trust?
5. Which service tier should be simplified or removed?
