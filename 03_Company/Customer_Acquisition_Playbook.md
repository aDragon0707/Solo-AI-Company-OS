## 3. Outreach Rules

First messages should be:

- short
- specific
- low pressure
- clear about the business question
- conservative about claims

First messages should not:

- attach raw source material
- imply certainty from unreviewed data
- pressure the recipient with alarming language
- promise a finding before work is scoped

---

## 4. Discovery Call Goal

The call should learn:

- what the customer wants to understand
- which [Vendor/Platform] or workflow matters
- what evidence would be useful
- what internal decision the output would support
- what timeline and budget are realistic

Do not turn the first call into a technical lecture.

---

## 5. Follow-Up Rule

After each meaningful customer interaction, update:

- account notes
- lead status
- next action
- any founder decision needed
- any AI handoff required

If it is not recorded, the company cannot operate from it.
