# Operating Manual

**Purpose:** Give the founder a practical operating model for running a solo company with AI employees.

---

## 1. The Three Company Layers

| Layer | Meaning | Founder Question |
|---|---|---|
| Truth Layer | What is verified, preserved, and reviewable | Is this real? |
| Interpretation Layer | What the company believes the truth means | Is this a fair conclusion? |
| Market Layer | What the company says to customers or partners | Is this safe to say? |

Do not collapse these layers. Market language must not outrank verified truth.

---

## 2. Standard Service Workflow

### Step 1: Define Scope

Confirm:

- [Target Account]
- [Industry]
- [Vendor/Platform]
- [Customer Segment]
- [Service Tier]
- timeline
- expected deliverables
- required founder approvals

### Step 2: Gather Source Material

Use the project-specific system to produce:

```text
[Data Capture Component] -> [Integrity Verification] -> [Analytical Output]
```

### Step 3: Review Claims

AI-04 reviews:

- what can be said
- what cannot be said
- what needs more verification
- what language is too strong

### Step 4: Prepare Customer-Facing Output

AI-03 prepares:

- one-page summary
- follow-up note
- offer or proposal draft
- account-specific message

### Step 5: Founder Approval

The founder approves:

- final customer language
- pricing
- scope
- public claims
- delivery promise

---

## 3. Weekly Company Rhythm

Each week, review:

- new qualified leads
- follow-ups sent
- customer replies
- discovery calls
- proposals
- paid work
- evidence or source-truth gaps
- founder decisions needed

If the company has no new customer conversations, it is not doing market validation.

---

## 4. Escalation Triggers

AI must escalate when:

- a customer asks for a promise outside approved scope
- a claim depends on unverified data
- pricing changes below approved baseline
- public language implies certainty not supported by truth
- legal, compliance, safety, financial, or medical sensitivity appears
- a draft conflicts with the founder decision log

---

## 5. Founder Operating Standard

The founder does not need to personally do every task.

The founder must own:

- decisions
- tradeoffs
- public commitments
- risk tolerance
- final narrative

AI employees do the work. The founder remains accountable for the company.
