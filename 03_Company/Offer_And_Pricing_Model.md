# Offer And Pricing Model

**Purpose:** Keep pricing and scope explicit so AI employees do not invent customer commitments.

---

## 1. Pricing Authority

Only the founder can approve:

- final pricing
- discounts
- delivery scope
- payment terms
- custom commitments

AI may draft pricing language, but it must not commit on the founder's behalf.

---

## 3. Scope Checklist

Before quoting, define:

- [Target Account]
- [Vendor/Platform]
- [Customer Segment]
- source material required
- analytical output expected
- review boundaries
- delivery format
- payment terms
- what is explicitly excluded

---

## 4. No-Scope-Creep Rule

If a customer asks for work outside the approved scope, AI must escalate to the founder.

Do not quietly absorb additional work into the current tier.
