# Worklog Index

**Purpose:** Track AI worklogs so the founder and AI employees know what was done, what is blocked, and who owns the next step.

---

## Naming Convention

```text
AI-XX_Worklog_YYYYMMDD_short-topic.md
```

Examples:

```text
AI-01_Worklog_YYYYMMDD_Daily_PMO.md
AI-02_Worklog_YYYYMMDD_Verification_Pass.md
AI-03_Worklog_YYYYMMDD_Outreach_Drafts.md
AI-04_Worklog_YYYYMMDD_Claim_Review.md
AI-05_Worklog_YYYYMMDD_Learning_Review.md
```
