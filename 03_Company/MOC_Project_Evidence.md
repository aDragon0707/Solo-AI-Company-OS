# MOC - Project Evidence

**Purpose:** Connect company-facing material to project truth without exposing proprietary implementation detail.

---

## Generic Evidence Workflow

Use this abstract chain:

```text
[Data Capture Component] -> [Integrity Verification] -> [Analytical Output]
```

---

## What Company Materials May Reference

Company materials may reference:

- verified status
- source availability
- reviewability
- scoped findings
- uncertainty and limitations
- approved analytical summaries

---

## What Company Materials Must Not Do

Company materials must not:

- expose sensitive implementation details
- present incomplete material as complete
- hide failed or uncertain status
- use a narrow sample as a universal conclusion
- make expert-sensitive conclusions without review

---

## Source Truth Rule

If a claim cannot be traced to a verified source, do not publish it.

Write `[Needs Verification]` instead of inventing support.
