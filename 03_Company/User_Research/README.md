# User Research

**Purpose:** Collect public pain signals and turn them into better onboarding, positioning, examples, and product decisions.

This folder is for market and user research about human-AI collaboration. It is not a place to copy private user data or paste full conversations.

## Research Rule

Use public discussions to understand language, pain, and workflow friction.

Do not:

- copy private messages
- include real names unless the source is intentionally public and attribution is necessary
- paste long copyrighted content
- treat upvotes as proof of truth
- turn one loud comment into a product decision

Every product-facing change still needs founder judgment.

---

## Best Signals

Good research signals usually describe:

- a repeated workflow failure
- a workaround people already use
- a phrase people naturally use for the problem
- a point where AI work becomes hard to manage
- a moment where solo operators feel stretched

Weak signals are vague excitement, launch posts with no discussion, or comments that only say a tool is cool.
