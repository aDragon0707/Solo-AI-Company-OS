# Company Workspace

**Purpose:** Manage company operations, AI employees, customer-facing assets, accounts, and worklogs without mixing them into founder decision ledgers or project source truth.

**Founder first reads:** `../FOUNDER_START_HERE.md`

---

## 1. What Belongs Here

Use this workspace for:

- company operating manuals
- product and service positioning
- customer acquisition planning
- offer and pricing models
- demo package drafts
- account workspaces
- AI employee prompts and coordination rules
- AI worklogs

Do not use this folder as the source of verified project truth. Company materials may summarize verified truth, but they must not overwrite it.

---

## 2. Company Workspace Map

| File or Folder | Purpose |
|---|---|
| `Operating_Manual.md` | How the founder runs the company system |
| `Product_Strategy.md` | Product, service, and market direction |
| `Customer_Acquisition_Playbook.md` | Prospecting, outreach, and sales process |
| `Offer_And_Pricing_Model.md` | Service tiers and pricing placeholders |
| `Demo_Package_Task_List.md` | Customer-facing asset checklist |
| `AI_Employees/` | Role files, start prompts, and coordination rules |
| `AI_Worklogs/` | Execution records and handoff trail |
| `Demo_Package/` | Reusable customer-facing templates |
| `Accounts/` | Target account templates |
| `MOC_*.md` | Obsidian navigation maps |

---

## 3. Company Truth Rule

Commercial materials are downstream of:

```text
[Data Capture Component] -> [Integrity Verification] -> [Analytical Output]
```

If the source truth is incomplete, the market-facing message must say so or avoid the claim.

---

## 4. Default Operating Loop

1. Founder reviews `01_Founder/FOUNDER_Decision_Log.md`.
2. AI-01 checks dashboards and worklogs.
3. AI-01 assigns work to the right AI employee.
4. The assigned AI completes the task and writes a worklog.
5. Any cross-role task includes a handoff section.
6. Founder confirms decisions, customer promises, and public claims.
